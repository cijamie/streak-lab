Protect

Paint over anything that must not move, like in Streak Lab: it stays exactly as photographed while the streak passes behind it. Erase to take paint away, or tap a person or object with Subject and an on-device AI model protects its outline.

Streak

Tap where the streak starts and drag the way it should go. The solid line across it is the slice: those pixels are pulled out into the streak. Bend curls it (positive curls to the left of its direction), Fan out widens it as it goes.
