Rotate

Drag the corners on the photo, or drag inside the frame to move it. The full photo is always kept, so you can change the crop later. Every export uses this crop.

Tone curve

Layers stack, so you can combine kinds, like rings around the moon plus streaks off a ridge. Pick the selected layer’s kind below.

Layer blending

Double-click (or double-tap) a slider’s name to reset it.

Protect settings

Use Brush or Subject on the photo. Protected areas stay free of streaks and warps, and Echo trails repeat what you protect.

Add a mask

A mask can brighten, cool or saturate one area, limit a streak layer (Streaks › Only inside), or limit the duotone (Grade › Apply).

Your saved looks

A look keeps your layers, sliders, colours and streak patterns so a whole series matches. Edges and protect paint stay with each photo.

Copy and paste

Paste onto another photo here, or onto several at once from the Library. Ctrl/⌘ Shift C and V work too.

Library

Photos and their edits are kept on this device only. Tap one to edit it, or use Select to paste settings onto several or export them together.
