How it works

Vertical and Radial: tap the photo to place the start line or the centre of the rings. Threshold decides how bright a pixel must be to streak; Length and Randomness shape the streaks.

Edge: drag along a ridge, treetop, outline or the rim of the moon. Streaks shoot out in the Direction you pick, and the white ticks show which way. Draw as many edges as you like.

Layers: add a layer to stack another mode on the same photo, such as Radial rings around the moon plus Edge streaks off a ridge. Each layer has its own mode, sliders and edges; the controls and the photo tools always work on the selected layer. Layers apply in order, so later layers streak the result of earlier ones. Colour, grain and protect paint apply once to the whole photo.

Protect (red) keeps the subject sharp. Erase removes protect paint and cuts away parts of edges. Hold Before to compare with the original.

Zoom: pinch with two fingers or scroll with a mouse or trackpad; + and − work too. Two fingers also move the photo; turn on Pan to move it with one finger or the mouse.

Shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes the last edge, hold \ to see the original.
